Such tools may record information such as how often you use the application, feature usage, viewing statistics, events that occur within the application, aggregated performance data, and where the application was downloaded from.

When you enable notifications, we use this to send you personalized short drama recommendations based on your viewing preferences. We strictly use this permission for delivering content updates about new or recommended short dramas and sending service-related notifications. We guarantee this permission will never be used for background data collection, cross-app tracking, or interruptive advertising. You retain full control and can manage notification preferences in your device settings at any time.
