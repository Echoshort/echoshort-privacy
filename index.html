Privacy Policy for EchoShort

Last Updated: April 20, 2026

Welcome to EchoShort. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our short-form drama streaming application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you register directly or via Social Logins (e.g., Google/Apple), we collect your email address, name, and user ID. Passwords are securely hashed and managed via Amazon Web Services (AWS) Cognito.
  • Usage & Viewing Data: To provide a personalized experience, we record your watch history, episode progress, bookmarks, and content preferences.
  • Virtual Currency & Transactions: We track your in-app "Coin" balances and subscription status. Note: We do not process or store your credit card information; all payments are handled securely through Apple In-App Purchases or Google Play Billing.
  • Device & Technical Data: We automatically collect device identifiers (such as Android ID or Advertising ID), IP addresses, and operating system versions to optimize video playback and serve relevant advertisements.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To keep EchoShort free or low-cost, we integrate third-party SDKs that may collect data according to their own policies:

  • AWS Cognito: For secure user authentication and identity management.
  • Google AdMob: For displaying rewarded and interstitial advertisements. AdMob may use your Advertising ID to serve personalized ads.
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics: To monitor app performance, fix crashes, and understand user behavior.

3. Data Deletion & Your Rights (CCPA / GDPR)

You have the right to request the complete deletion of your account, personal data, and viewing history.

How to delete your data: You can permanently delete your account directly inside the EchoShort app by navigating to Profile > Settings > Delete Account.

Upon deletion, your AWS identity, watch history, coin balance, and local database records are immediately and permanently erased from our active servers. (Please note: Unused coins are forfeited upon account deletion and cannot be refunded.)

4. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, including HTTPS encryption in transit, encrypted token storage via the Android Keystore, and secure backend APIs, to protect your personal information from unauthorized access.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our app or legal requirements. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.
